Important: These are general exam-day guidelines. Always read your current admit card and the official instructions issued by the examination authority. If any rule differs, the official instructions will take priority.

Documents to Carry

  • Admit Card: Carry a clear printed copy of your admit card. Check whether colour or black-and-white printing is specified.
  • Photo ID: Carry the original, valid photo identity document mentioned in the admit card.
  • Photographs: Carry recent passport-size photographs if required by the exam authority.

Dress Code & Prohibited Items

  • Accessories: Avoid jewellery, watches, smart devices, and unnecessary accessories.
  • Electronic Items: Do not carry mobile phones, calculators, Bluetooth devices, or other prohibited electronics.
  • Metal Items: Avoid belts, large buckles, or metal accessories if restricted by the exam instructions.
  • Simple Clothing: Wear comfortable, uncomplicated clothing and follow the exam-specific dress code.
