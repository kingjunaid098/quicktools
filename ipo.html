IPO Profit & Settlement Calculator

Calculate IPO investment, profit allocation, account holder share, reinvestment, and final settlement.

1. Current IPO Inputs

By default, the account holder's percentage is deducted directly from gross profit. Enter a custom amount to use a fixed amount instead. The custom amount overrides the percentage. No share is deducted when there is a loss.

Example:
Bought for ₹15,000 and sold for ₹17,000.
Gross Profit = ₹2,000
Account Holder Share at 10% = ₹200
Your Profit After Share = ₹1,800
