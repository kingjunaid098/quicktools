1. Current IPO Inputs

The account holder's percentage is deducted directly from your gross profit. For example, 10% of ₹2,000 profit is ₹200. No share is deducted when there is a loss.

Example:
IPO bought for: ₹15,000
IPO sold for: ₹17,000
Gross profit: ₹2,000
Account holder share (10%): ₹200
Your profit after share: ₹1,800
Total capital available: ₹16,800
